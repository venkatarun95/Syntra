Search CCA in cc_sim

The Syntra CCA with a live minimax search every round (depth 4 with quiescence on unreported loss; no distilled rule): its exact QE belief over C, B and the queue, K hypotheses, change detection, and a planner that only picks the objective. Run over cc_sim's packet-level links on a focus set of ten cases, each a little past convergence, against Reno, Copa and the ack-window CCA (window = the acks of the last g min-RTTs, at least 4 packets). One control round is one handshake RTT (longer on slow links); the CCA picks its own units so that C stays inside the model's prior of 5 to 1000 units per round.

What the runs show

The CCA searches to depth 4 and extends a leaf by up to 4 plies while loss it has risked is still unreported (quiescence; offering only the no-loss rate against every network reply). Cruise holds at min_c; draining happens only in Drain, when the queue has not been provably empty for 40 rounds. Previous run (plain depth 6, Cruise drain candidate) in brackets.

  • Startup keeps to the loss budget. Static 12 Mbit/s: 28 packets lost on 0.5 BDP (40), 19 on 1 BDP (60), 0 on 4 BDP; the budget while C is uncertain is a BDP, about 48 packets. Depth 6 broke it on 1 BDP in an earlier run (89): the loss of stacked probes was reported 3–4 rounds after sending, beyond the tree.
  • Cruise fills the link. After warm-up, utilization is 0.98–1.00 on every constant link (4 BDP 0.985, was 0.94), at 1 Gbit/s, after both steps, and with K=4 and K=16 ack bursts; no loss after warm-up anywhere but the cellular traces.
  • Whole run: static 0.95 / 0.33–0.49% loss (0.93 / 0.7–1.1%); step up 0.92 / 1.1% (0.87 / 1.4%), the best of the five CCAs; step down 0.87 / 3.1% (0.82 / 3.7%); Poisson 0.93 / 1.0% (0.92 / 3.9%), 3 restarts (8); K bursts 0.94 / 0.25–0.62%.
  • Cellular is unchanged (TMobile 9.6%, ATT 17.2% loss): almost all of it is in Recover, the rule mode after a belief restart, which has no QE window to search with.
  • 1 Gbit/s startup is slower: 2.9 s to reach 90% (2.2 s), utilization 0.58 over the 6 s run.

Scenarios in detail

Reading the decisions

Every round the CCA reports what it sent, what the receiver acknowledged and what was lost, and the engine updates its belief: intervals for the link rate C, the buffer B and the queue, which provably contain the true values if the link behaves like the model (a constant-rate link that may serve up to a round late, with arrivals in bursts of up to K). The planner only picks a mode, which sets the objective and the candidate rates; a depth-4 minimax search (two rounds against the worst-case link, extended while risked loss is unreported) picks the rate. Every rate keeps the worst-case queue within one BDP; loss is budgeted over the last 20 rounds; the upper bound on C comes from the last 20 rounds only, so a rise in C gets looked for again. The text under the charts (and the hover) gives each round's reason.
