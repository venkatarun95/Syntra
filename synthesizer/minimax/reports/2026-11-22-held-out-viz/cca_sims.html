Minimax CCA in simulation

Seven simulated links, 100 RTTs each, driven by the synthesized congestion controller: an exact belief over the link's capacity C and buffer B, a planner that picks a mode each RTT, and a minimax search among the moves the mode allows. One time step is one minimum RTT; rates are packets per RTT.
